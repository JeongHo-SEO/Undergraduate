I mainly studied Artificial Intelligence on [Daegu Gyeongbuk Institute of Science and Technology](https://www.dgist.ac.kr/), simply DGIST.

To fulfill my interest, I completed several specialized courses at partner universities that were not available in the standard DGIST curriculum.

  - [2022-summer] Enrolled in [Department of Mathematical Sciences](https://www.math.snu.ac.kr/)
  - [2025-spring] Enrolled in [Department of Statistics](https://stat.snu.ac.kr/)
2. **[🇫🇷 Grenoble INP - UGA](https://www.grenoble-inp.fr/)**

Morevoer, I participated the *General Online Courses* Program which supplies academic credits for [edX](https://www.edx.org/) and [Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/) courses including certificates.

- [2025-summer]: edX for a statistics course
